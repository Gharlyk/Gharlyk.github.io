This site is where I'll be posting writeups of the projects I build in my spare time — a lot of them with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) as a collaborator. The idea: whenever I finish (or reach a good milestone on) something, I write up what it does, how it works, and what I learned building it, and link to the full code on [my GitHub](https://github.com/Gharlyk).

Partly this is a portfolio — a way to show what I've built. Partly it's for anyone else poking at similar problems: if a writeup here saves someone an afternoon, that's the point.

More soon.
